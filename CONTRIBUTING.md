# Contributing

Contributions improve evidence coverage, attribution, bibliographic accuracy, identity resolution, taxonomy, methodology, accessibility, and tooling. They do not create an entitlement to inclusion or a backlink.

Use the issue forms for one source, book, person correction, role correction, bibliographic correction, statement correction, rights or privacy concern, taxonomy proposal, dossier proposal, research batch, translation correction, or conflict disclosure. Include canonical URLs and state which facts each source supports.

Research changes require a dedicated branch, registered protocol and batch, prompt ID and version, access and rights basis, accepted/rejected/held logs, duplicate and concentration review, AI-assistance disclosure, validation results, limitations, and human-review state. Leave human approval pending unless a named human actually reviewed the work.

Schema, methodology, rights-policy, and production-record changes require CODEOWNER review. Do not commit secrets, private source material, full third-party transcripts, generated private review notes, or staging data to Pages.
