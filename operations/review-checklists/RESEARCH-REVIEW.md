# Research review checklist

Verify canonical identity, authorship or speaker, role at source time, dates, original publisher, canonical URL, access basis, rights, ownership, statement type, neutral paraphrase, exact locator, scope, uncertainty, topic IDs, source diversity, concentration, conflicts, and OFF relationship. Compare the proposed and previous record. Record corrections, disagreements, unresolved issues, and publication recommendation. Never infer human approval from absence of objections.
