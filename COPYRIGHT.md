Full transcripts may be published only for OFF-owned material, public-domain or openly licensed material, or material with documented permission. Access for research does not itself authorize republication. Third-party names, metadata, trademarks, works, source text, reports, transcripts, and media are not relicensed by the repository's licenses.

Affiliate links are not used. Rights complaints enter the correction workflow and block affected publication while unresolved.
