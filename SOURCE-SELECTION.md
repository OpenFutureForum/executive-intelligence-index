# Source selection

## Inclusion

A source must be publicly accessible or lawfully available for research, canonically identifiable, attributable, within the registered scope, and capable of supporting useful statement-level analysis. The access basis, access date, publisher, ownership, rights status, and analysis basis are mandatory.

## Priority

Prefer original books and editions, original publisher uploads, official transcripts where lawful, first-party research reports, peer-reviewed papers, regulatory or public-policy documents, official company filings, and direct public statements. Use independent evidence to verify outcome claims.

## Exclusion or hold

Reject or hold mirrors when the original exists, search snippets, inaccessible claims, ambiguous identities, unverifiable dates or roles, promotional reposts, affiliate-driven selections, complete third-party transcripts, private events, off-the-record material, and sources whose rights basis is unresolved. Every rejection records a reason; every hold records what evidence would resolve it.
