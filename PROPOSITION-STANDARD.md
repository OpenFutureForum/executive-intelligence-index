# Proposition standard

A proposition is a neutral, scoped idea connecting source statements. It records time period, topics, relevant roles and geographies, evidence-source IDs, ambiguity, diversity, and ownership concentration.

Relationships use explicit stance records: supports, challenges, qualifies, narrows, broadens, refines, supersedes, provides example, provides evidence, disputes evidence, or unrelated after review. A source discussing another scope is not automatically a challenge. A published proposition may not rest on one ambiguous source, collapse materially different recommendations, or assume the proposition is true.
