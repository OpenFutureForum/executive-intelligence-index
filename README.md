# Open Executive Intelligence Index

**Trace executive ideas to their sources.**

The Open Executive Intelligence Index is an Open Future Forum research project designed to connect selected books, reports, studies, talks, interviews and media to source-located statements, carefully scoped propositions and evidence-backed editorial synthesis.

## Public status

The human-readable project overview is published at:

https://openfutureforum.github.io/executive-intelligence-index/

This is a **pre-release framework overview**. It publishes no production research records and makes no claim that staged or candidate material has passed human editorial review. Research branches, staging files, review material and synthetic fixtures are not public evidence.

## Publication boundaries

- Every public statement must be attributable to a reproducible source location.
- A recorded statement is not automatically treated as true, representative or independently verified.
- Role analysis uses the person's role at source time.
- OFF-owned sources and relationships must be disclosed.
- Engagement is kept separate from evidence quality.
- Named human approval is required before a research release is presented as published.
- Copyrighted works are cited and summarized within defined limits, not republished.
