# Evidence standard

The index distinguishes a statement made by a speaker, a finding reported by a study, a verified external fact, a researcher interpretation, and an editorial conclusion. Predictions remain predictions; opinions remain opinions; company claims remain attributed company claims.

Research records preserve study design, sample, population, geography, dates, funding, sponsor, peer-review status, methodology quality, findings, limitations, corrections, and retractions when the source discloses them. Missing information is recorded as unknown, not inferred.

Evidence quality dimensions remain separate. No composite score substitutes for reading the source, and high source frequency is not treated as proof.
