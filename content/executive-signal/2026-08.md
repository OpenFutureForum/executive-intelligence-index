## 2. Reporting-period methodology

The reporting period ends on the date of the available deterministic release artifact, 2026-08-14. The immediately preceding completed calendar month is used as the comparison period. Eligibility follows `METHODOLOGY.md`, `EVIDENCE-STANDARD.md`, `config/trend-methodology.yml`, and the publication gates:

- only canonical records that are published, named-human approved, and included in a frozen release may contribute evidence;
- staging records, unresolved corrections, duplicated reposts, metadata-only changes, synthetic fixtures, and records without comparable dates are excluded;
- raw values, denominators, default weights, normalized values, and limitations are disclosed separately;
- a comparison requires both a current and a previous frozen release;
- engagement is separated from evidence strength.

The current release fingerprint was inspected, along with its public record counts and limitations. No earlier approved or deterministic frozen release was found in the repository history available to this branch. Because the comparison denominator is absent, default methodology weights are disclosed but not applied; normalized values and the composite score remain null.
