# Governance

Open Future Forum is the publisher and final editorial authority. Maintainers steward code, schemas, vocabularies, research batches, rights review, and releases through repository history and CODEOWNERS.

Schema versions change only when the record contract changes. Taxonomy additions begin in `staging/proposed-taxonomy/`. Production releases require passing checks and the configured human approvals. Automated agents may prepare changes and machine-review states but cannot approve human-review fields or merge their own work.

Corrections, withdrawals, migrations, prompt versions, batch manifests, and release fingerprints preserve accountability. Material policy changes are recorded in `DECISIONS.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`.
