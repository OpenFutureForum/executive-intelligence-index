# Corrections

Submit the affected record or page, field, proposed correction, evidence, and whether the concern involves attribution, bibliography, current role, rights, privacy, translation, or data integrity. Maintainers record the decision, reason, previous and new values, reviewer, correction date, and public note.

Accepted corrections preserve history through revision and correction records. Rejected requests retain a reason. Material rights or privacy concerns block publication until resolved. Withdrawn records remain identifiable so retired IDs are not reused.
