# Limitations

- All records are staging candidates produced by machine-assisted normalization; named human review is pending.
- The 46 statements inherit human-review-pending status from BATCH-2026-006, although they were independently machine checked.
- The corpus contains eight intellectual sources with statements. It is below the 10-source standard-topic threshold and far below the full-dossier threshold.
- No debate record was created: the only apparent control tension had four relevant statements from one intellectual source, below the configured 12-statement and two-position threshold.
- Normative standards and governance reports dominate the identity-control propositions; the sources do not compare implementations or outcomes.
- Security findings come from synthetic or sandboxed benchmarks and cannot estimate production prevalence.
- Geography is weak: normative sources use United States or global contexts, while benchmark studies have no human geography.
- Role tags indicate executive relevance, not measured role-specific views; there are no direct executive interviews in the supplied corpus.
- Vendor-source presence is disclosed, and source count is not treated as truth.
- The repository contains no canonical proposition or debate records beyond README files, so overlap checks are internal to this batch.
- Two broad generative-AI statements remain held because they do not support an agent-specific, multi-source proposition.
