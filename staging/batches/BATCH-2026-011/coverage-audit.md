# Global coverage audit — Governed identities for AI agents

## Outcome

The verified production corpus is empty. No region, country, language, role, industry, organization type, or source type is represented in production. The staged research corpus contains eight distinct intellectual works and 46 statements, all in English and all awaiting human review. Global-applicability labels on standards and policy sources are not counted as regional representation.

This batch creates a discovery-only queue of 60 locally grounded institutional candidates across 7 regions, 33 countries or regional jurisdictions, and 14 original-language identifiers. 18 pass machine screening, 40 require topical, identity, or language resolution, and 2 are rejected. No production record or regional page was created.

## Concentration findings

- Production has no evidence and therefore no defensible representation claim.
- The staged intellectual-work corpus is 100% English.
- US policy sources account for 2 of 8 distinct staged works (25%); three standards or policy works are labeled global, but that does not establish local coverage.
- Three of 8 staged works use synthetic or non-human-geography settings.
- Role tags exist, but direct executive role-at-source-time evidence is zero.
- The staged corpus has no OFF-owned source. Vendor involvement is present in two of eight distinct works (25%) when academic–industry AgentDojo and OpenAI are counted; consulting and investor sources are zero.
- The material gap is not merely geographic search visibility: it is missing local operators, regulators, executive roles, industries, study populations, and native-language verification.

## Candidate queue interpretation

22 candidates are screened as directly about agentic AI or governed agent identity. The remaining candidates are adjacent national or regional AI-governance sources and are generally held until section-level relevance is verified. Every candidate is staging-only and needs human selection.

## Representation rule

A source is locally grounded when its originating institution and intended policy, study, or operating context are local. That does not make a single source representative of a region. Person geography, organization headquarters, operating markets, publication geography, event geography, market discussed, study geography, regulatory geography, and audience geography remain separate fields.
