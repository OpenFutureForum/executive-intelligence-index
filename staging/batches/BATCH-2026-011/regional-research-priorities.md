# Regional research priorities

1. **Native-language direct-source review:** French, Spanish, Finnish, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Thai, Indonesian, Italian, Danish, German, and Brazilian Portuguese candidates. Verify titles, editions, relevant sections, and quotation restrictions.
2. **Role-at-source-time evidence:** recruit or locate local CISO, CIO, CTO, GC, board, CFO, founder, PE, and VC primary sources. Do not backfill current roles.
3. **Operator evidence:** prioritize local deployments in financial services, critical infrastructure, healthcare, public administration, and technology platforms.
4. **Undercovered regions:** Africa, Latin America and the Caribbean, and the Middle East need direct agent-identity evidence rather than general national strategies.
5. **Asia depth:** Singapore and Japan have the strongest current direct queue; South Korea, India, China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Thailand, and Indonesia need locally verified operator and regulator follow-up.
6. **Publication discipline:** no regional briefing until at least two people, two organizations, and verified region-specific production sources satisfy the repository threshold.
