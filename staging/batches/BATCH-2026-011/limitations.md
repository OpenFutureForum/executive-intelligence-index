# Limitations

- The prompt placeholders were unresolved. This batch uses the continuing topic **Governed identities for AI agents** and batch **BATCH-2026-011** based on the repository’s active research sequence.
- Production is empty. Staging records are not production evidence, and candidate records are not verified sources.
- Candidate discovery relies on public web visibility and official-source indexing as of 2026-08-15; it is not exhaustive.
- Machine screening can miss local-language nuance, policy status, superseded editions, or source identity changes. Human review is pending for every record.
- Machine-assisted English title translations are metadata aids only. No translated summary or quotation is approved.
- Geographic fields describe institutional grounding and intended context; they do not establish population representativeness.
- National strategies often provide adjacent governance context but not direct evidence about governed agent identities. These are held unless direct relevance was visible.
- The queue is intentionally public-authority heavy to correct the lack of local regulatory context. It does not solve the operator, CFO, investor, founder, or executive-primary-source gaps.
- The South African March 2026 draft policy is rejected because the government withdrew it following concerns about fabricated and unverifiable sources; the 2024 precursor remains held pending policy-history review.
- No regional page meets publication thresholds. Candidate counts cannot satisfy production gates.
