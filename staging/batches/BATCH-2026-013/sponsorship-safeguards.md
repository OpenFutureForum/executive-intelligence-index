# Sponsorship safeguards

These safeguards apply to every interview, cross-role discussion, survey, roundtable, panel, video, and flagship report in `BATCH-2026-013`.

## Required separation

- Sponsors may fund logistics, production, venue, translation, recruitment administration, or a clearly labeled unrestricted research grant.
- Sponsors may not select participants, set or veto questions, suppress negative results, edit analysis, approve findings, delay publication, or purchase inclusion in a benchmark, registry, panel, or report.
- OFF retains control of the protocol, sampling plan, instrument, analysis, interpretation, corrections, and release timing.
- Research staff must disclose OFF ownership, sponsor category, paid research relationships, Murray Newlands’s relevant advisory or investment relationships, and any participant commercial relationship material to the topic.
- Vendor, advisor, investor, buyer, operator, regulator, and civil-society records remain distinct.

## Data-access limits

- Sponsors receive only the public report and any aggregate appendix promised to all readers.
- No sponsor receives identifiable responses, interview recordings, private transcripts, attendee lists, contact data, row-level survey exports, small cells, unpublished quotes, or off-the-record material.
- Any early factual review is limited to the sponsor’s own attributed description and creates no right to alter findings.
- Minimum-cell and suppression rules apply before release; secure retention and deletion periods must be documented.

## Disclosure template

Each output must state the sponsor category, funding purpose, material commercial relationship, data-access limit, and this sentence in substance: “The sponsor did not select participants, shape the instrument, analyze the data, approve the findings, or control publication.”

## Conflict risk by category

| Sponsor category | Primary conflict risk | Required control |
|---|---|---|
| Identity, security, or observability vendor | Research could imply product endorsement | No rankings; attributed vendor sample; independent operator verification; no pay-to-include |
| Cloud or AI platform | Platform-specific framing and benchmark influence | Multi-platform design; version disclosure; no exclusive method control |
| Audit, legal, or consulting firm | Advice may be presented as neutral evidence | Distinguish professional interpretation from outcome evidence; disclose client-development interest |
| Insurer or broker | Loss-model and control requirements may favor underwriting products | Publish assumptions; include buyer and policyholder views; no product recommendation |
| Bank or financial-services provider | Budget findings may be used for commercial positioning | No access to respondent-level finance data; independent analysis |
| Executive-search or community platform | Recruitment incentives and title inflation | Verify role at source time; report recruitment channels; no attendee-list access |
| Investment firm | Portfolio promotion and thesis confirmation | No named portfolio preference; disclose holdings and advisory relationships; separate investor from operator evidence |
| Standards or professional association | Institutional position may dominate framing | Include dissent and implementation evidence; do not treat membership as adoption |

Concept files identify the most suitable category and specific risk for each proposed output. Category suitability is not an endorsement or an assertion that any sponsor has agreed.
