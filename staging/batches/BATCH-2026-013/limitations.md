# Limitations

- The production corpus for the topic is empty; the agenda is derived from documented staging gaps, not published findings.
- Proposition, statement, debate, role, regional, and signal inputs remain human-review pending unless explicitly described as public OFF overlap.
- Existing OFF reports and pages are OFF-owned, selective-community material and are not independent verification of OFF claims.
- Public OFF pages were checked for overlap and format availability; private dinner content, target names, attendee lists, and unpublished sponsor information were not accessed or used.
- No proposed participant, interviewer, moderator, partner, or sponsor is named or implied to have agreed.
- Scores are separate editorial judgments on a 1–5 scale; they are not measured quantities and are not aggregated.
- Editorial tiers do not represent a composite ranking, probability of success, or publication commitment.
- OFF access creates selection bias. Role, industry, geography, company size, event, referral, and response effects must be disclosed.
- Role relevance tags are not role-at-source-time evidence.
- Surveys can describe their recruited respondents but cannot estimate universal executive prevalence without an appropriate sampling frame.
- Matched organizational cases may be hard to recruit and may overrepresent organizations with mature governance or positive stories.
- Negative cases, abandoned controls, incidents, and non-adopters are likely harder to obtain and require deliberate recruitment.
- Confidential roundtables can generate questions and themes but cannot produce attributable evidence without separate, explicit research consent.
- Translation and cross-jurisdiction work requires qualified reviewers and locally informed legal interpretation.
- Sponsor interest may be highest where conflict risk is also highest; the firewall in `sponsorship-safeguards.md` is mandatory.
- Video and panel formats favor concise positions and may flatten scope; companion pages must preserve methods, counterpoints, and limitations.
- No schedule, budget, sample, or publication threshold is approved until a named human reviews feasibility, consent, and resourcing.
