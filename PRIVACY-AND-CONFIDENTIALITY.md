# Privacy and confidentiality

Private Forum Select discussions, off-the-record dinners, confidential roundtables, private attendee lists, personal email addresses, private phone numbers or addresses, private calendars, unpublished sponsor discussions, and confidential partner information are excluded without explicit documented permission.

Public professional information may be indexed when relevant to a public source. Sensitive personal characteristics are not inferred. Public event recordings may be indexed according to their rights status. Participants may submit attribution corrections. The privacy validator scans canonical and public content before deployment.
