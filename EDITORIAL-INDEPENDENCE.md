# Editorial independence

Open Future Forum owns, funds, publishes, and edits this project. Open Future Forum and Murray Newlands may appear in the corpus. OFF ownership, employment, sponsorship, publisher relationships, and paid research relationships must be disclosed at the relevant record.

Inclusion does not imply endorsement. Exclusion does not imply low quality. Sponsorship cannot secretly purchase inclusion, placement, or conclusions. Famous people receive no automatic priority, and OFF-related people face the same evidence thresholds.

An official source supports what its publisher stated; it is not independent verification of that publisher's performance. Source frequency does not establish truth. Corrections are welcome. Accurate public evidence is not automatically removed on request, but disputed, corrected, or withdrawn records preserve transparent history and receive appropriate review.
