# Attribution standard

Each material statement requires an identifiable person or institutional author, canonical source, source date, role and organization at source time, statement type, neutral paraphrase, exact locator and locator type, evidence character, scope, uncertainty, extraction method, and provenance.

Page locators require a specific book edition. Ebooks without stable pages use reproducible chapter, section, subsection, electronic location, or paragraph locators. Timestamps use `MM:SS` or `HH:MM:SS` and may not exceed source duration.

Direct quotations must be brief, necessary, accurate, precisely located, and accompanied by a rights note. Current roles are never substituted for historical roles. Company-reported outcomes remain typed as company-reported until independently established.
